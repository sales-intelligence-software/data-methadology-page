02 · Methodology

How signals become opportunities

Three steps turn scattered research activity into a clean, actionable account list.

1

Collect

We pull intent signals for the topics you choose, including selected Bombora categories.

2

Merge & de-duplicate

Signals are matched to the same company and de-duplicated so one account is never counted twice.

3

Match your network

Each account is checked against your LinkedIn connections to find the shortest, strongest path to a likely decision maker.

06 · Accuracy & limits

Strong signal.
Not a guarantee.

Intent data is most useful when you understand exactly what it does and does not tell you.

Privacy & compliance
Third-party intent data is aggregated at the company level. Where personal data is involved, providers rely on consent collected through publisher networks and support opt-out requests.
✓Shows company-level research activity, not that a specific person is ready to buy.
✓Some noise is unavoidable because students, competitors and researchers may also read about topics.
✓Smaller companies create fewer signals and may appear less often.
✓A warm path makes an introduction easier, but the person still decides whether to help.
✓BlueChimp combines intent, relationships and buyer fit rather than relying on a single signal.
07 · FAQ

Questions about the methodology

Do you track individual people?

No. Intent signals are company-level. Relationship paths come only from your own LinkedIn connections.

Which intent topics can I track?

You choose the topics during setup, including Bombora intent categories that match what you sell.

How is the intent score calculated?

It combines the strength and recency of research activity across sources into a 0 to 100 score.

Do you store my CSV uploads?

No. Files are parsed in your browser and never saved to BlueChimp servers.
